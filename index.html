HOW TO PLAY

  • ⬍Move one cell at a time — swipe on mobile, or arrow keys / WASD on desktop.
  • ◈Echoes are ghosts of your past moves. They appear over time and retrace the exact path you walked.
  • ✕Avoid them. Step onto any echo and it's game over. The board tightens as more spawn.
  • ★Score +1 for every move you survive. Outlast yourself and beat your best.
